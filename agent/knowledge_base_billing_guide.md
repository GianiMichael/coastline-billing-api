# Coastline Retail Co. Telecom Billing Guide

This guide explains how telecom services are billed for Coastline Retail store locations. All figures are monthly unless noted.

## Standard services at every store

Each store has the same core package:

| Service | Carrier | Standard monthly cost |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Business internet, 500 Mbps fiber | Brightline Fiber | $189 |
| Backup internet, 4G/5G wireless failover | Summit Wireless | $45 |
| Phone lines, 4 cloud voice lines | Brightline Voice | $32 per line ($128 total) |
| Point of sale data line | Brightline Fiber | $60 |
| Manager mobile phones, 2 lines | Summit Wireless | $55 per line ($110 total) |

Standard total for a store: about $532 per month before taxes and fees.

## What each line item means

- **Monthly recurring charge (MRC):** the fixed monthly price for a service.
- **Non recurring charge (NRC):** a one time charge, such as installation, equipment, or a technician visit.
- **Usage charge:** a charge based on use, such as long distance minutes or data overage.
- **Prorated charge:** a partial month charge when a service starts, stops, or changes mid cycle. Prorated charges often make one bill look higher, then the next bill returns to normal.
- **Taxes, surcharges, and regulatory fees:** government and carrier fees, usually 12 to 18 percent of the bill. These change with the services on the bill.
- **Late fee:** a 1.5 percent charge on any balance unpaid after 30 days.

## Common reasons a bill goes up

1. **Promotional pricing ended.** Many services start with a 12 month discount. When it ends, the price returns to the standard rate.
2. **Prorated charges.** A service added or changed mid month creates a one time partial charge.
3. **One time charges.** Installation, a technician visit ($150 per visit), or replacement equipment.
4. **Data overage.** Backup wireless or manager phones that go over their data plan are billed $15 per extra gigabyte.
5. **Annual price increase.** Brightline raises prices up to 5 percent each January, as allowed in the contract.
6. **Duplicate or unused services.** A line that was supposed to be disconnected is still billing.

## Unused and savings opportunities

- A phone line with **zero calls for 60 days or more** is considered unused and can be reviewed for disconnection.
- A manager mobile line with **no data use for 60 days** is considered unused.
- Stores that closed or relocated may still have services billing at the old address.
- The agent can flag these, but only the telecom team can disconnect a service.

## Disputes and escalation

- Billing disputes must be raised within **60 days** of the invoice date.
- Disputes over **$500** go to the corporate telecom team.
- The corporate telecom team responds to escalations within **2 business days**.
- To escalate, the agent logs the store number, caller name, issue summary, and amount in question.

## Billing cycle

- All carriers bill on the **1st of each month**, with payment due on the **30th**.
- Invoices are paid centrally by Coastline corporate finance, not by individual stores.
